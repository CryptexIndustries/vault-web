Cryptex Vault stores passwords, passkeys and verification codes in an encrypted vault on your device. Create and use a local vault without an account.

<b>Manage your credentials</b>
<ul>
<li>Organize entries into directories and search your vault.</li>
<li>Generate passwords and use Android autofill.</li>
<li>Store passkeys and time-based verification codes.</li>
<li>Unlock with your master password or configured biometrics.</li>
<li>Import, export and keep a recovery code for your vault.</li>
</ul>

<b>Link your devices</b>
Create or use an invitation with a QR code or invitation file. Linked vaults can synchronize with Cryptex Vault on Android, the web and supported browser extensions. Device linking and synchronization use encrypted transfers.

<b>Optional Online Services</b>
Online Services offers a paid monthly or yearly subscription for managed encrypted backups, hosted signaling and relay services, and account device management. Local vault use does not require a subscription. Account creation uses Cloudflare Turnstile, and billing opens Stripe Checkout in your browser. These optional features use external network services.
